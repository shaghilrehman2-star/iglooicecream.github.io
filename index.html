About Us

Founded in 1993 by Mr. Lutfur Rehman, Igloo Ice-cream & Co. was born from a simple vision — to create exceptional ice cream using the finest ingredients and the freshest dairy cream. What began as a small passion-driven venture has grown into a trusted name known for quality, consistency, and unforgettable taste. For over three decades, we have blended tradition with innovation to craft rich, creamy, and exotic flavors that delight every generation. Each scoop reflects our commitment to excellence, authenticity, and uncompromising standards. At Igloo Ice-cream & Co., we believe ice cream is more than a dessert — it’s an experience of happiness, crafted with care and served with pride.
